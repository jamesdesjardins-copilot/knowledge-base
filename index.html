HOW TO: Add a Certification to an SHSM Student Select Courses and Programs → All Programs → Find the SHSM Sector pertaining to you school and click on Display → Choose a student Click on Compliance Click on SHSM Certification Click on Add new certification for current programs only Enter in details Click on submit HOW TO: Print the Specialist High Skills Major Student Record Card for Students Who Have Completed the Program If you are printing specific students’ records, ensure they are first loaded into your selection screen before continuing; if you are printing all SHSM student records, proceed to step 2 Select Data & Reporting → Reports → Compliance Reports Click on Specialist High Skills Major Record If you are printing specific students’ records, select ‘The selected X students only’; if you are printing all SHSM student records, Click on ‘All Students’ Choose which SHSM Program from drop down menu or select ‘All Programs’ Choose SHSM requirements (‘students who have completed all requirements’, ‘students who have not completed all requirements’, or ‘all students’) Choose an ‘Effective Date’ (usually the current date–it does not default to this). Please note: if effective date is set for a date AFTER the exit date of a student, a blank report will be generated; if an effective date is set for a date BEFORE certain program elements were completed, those elements will not populate. Thus, the BEST time to run/print these reports is at the end of June once all course attainment and program elements have been posted in Powerschool but before the end of the school year / a posted exit date for the student NOTE: IN ORDER FOR THE BOX LABELED ‘This student has met the requirements of the Ontario Secondary School Diploma‘ Checked, the following criteria must be satisfied: The student has all required credits, certifications, a Reach Ahead, an SPE, and an Experiential Learning Opportunity checked/entered/completed in PS SIS The ‘All Requirements Met’ date must be populated for the student The ‘Diploma Certificate Requirements Met’ date (found under Compliance->General Information->Graduation) as well as the ‘All Requirements Met’ date for SHSM components must be set for a date at least 1 day PRIOR to the student’s exit date for SHSM; this is used particularly for students who have already graduated but are returning for a copy of an SHSM Major Record; please note: both of these dates are calculated by running a report–they are NOT manual entry fields Click ‘Run Now’ Click ‘Submit’ Please note: if printing for a student who has already graduated, the ‘Effective Date’ should be set to the same date as the student’s ‘Diploma Certificate Requirements Met Date’ date (Compliance->General Information->Graduation). HOW TO: Graduate a Student (All Requirements Met) At the end of the school year, a member of the commencement team must process step 6 on the End of Term and Final Reporting Processes provided by LTSS. Namely, the report entitled ‘All Requirements Met Date Calculation’ must be run to populate the ‘All Requirements Met’ date field on the SHSM Edit Special Program Enrolment page in PS-SIS. Run this BEFORE OSSD Diploma Date Calculation. Note – this is a calculation and will not produce a standard report. All SHSM requirements must be entered. Data & Reporting > Reports > Compliance Reports > (Special Functions) SHSM All Requirements Met Date Calculation. DO NOT ENTER THIS DATE MANUALLY. Ensure that all SHSM students you wish to designate as ‘all requirements met’ are selected on the student selection page Select the first student from the student selection page Select Courses and Programs → School Programs → Click on the ‘Entry Date’ for the applicable SHSM program Ensure that a student has completed all requirements for the SHSM program; this includes: A bundle of credits that apply to the student’s chosen pathway (workplace, apprenticeship, college, or university; pathway is determined by grade 11 English credit (ex. 3U=university, etc.). These include: 4 Applicable Major Courses (or acceptable substitutes) Applicable English Course(s) Applicable Math Course(s) (if required) Applicable Other Course(s) (if required) (or acceptable substitutes) A minimum of 2 Credits of Co-op All required Certifications (both ‘compulsory’ and ‘optional/elective’) An Experiential Learning Opportunity A Sector Partnered Experience A Reach Ahead Experience Ensure the ‘All Requirements Met Date’ has been populated by the aforementioned report run in step 1 POPULATE THE EXIT DATE BOX WITH A DATE THAT IS AT LEAST 1 DAY AFTER THE ‘ALL REQUIREMENTS MET’ DATE AND THE ‘DIPLOMA CERTIFICATE REQUIREMENTS MET’ DATE (found under Compliance->General Information->Graduation). This could be 1 day after the final day of the semester. This exit date should be entered at the end of the semester right before printing of report cards, transcripts, and/or SHSM Major records, not before; an exit date should NOT be inputted as a post-dated entry when a student is registered into an SHSM program or any other time during the school year. An incorrect date could create incorrect ONSIS reporting information and therefore have impacts on Ministry SHSM success completion reporting (and, in the long-term, would therefore have negative impacts to future budgeting). Click on submit. HOW TO: Remove (End date) a Student from SHSM For students who are unsuccessful in attaining their red seal in SHSM OR for students who wish to leave the program before it is completed, an ‘Exit Date’ must be entered; DO NOT COMPLETE A DATE FOR ‘ALL REQUIREMENTS MET’; leave that field blank or as 0/0/0 NOTE: DO NOT DELETE THE SHSM PROGRAM FOR THE STUDENT!
